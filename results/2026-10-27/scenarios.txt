Scenarios (all: 3 ms RTT + up to 2x200 us jitter, 80 Mbit/s, MTU 1280, 2.8 MB fetches, 3 s idle before each):
  default (gvisor-*.txt, linux-kernel.txt): -trials 8 -trace
      fresh connection per trial, 2 warm-up fetches, then deliver 1000 packets and drop
      everything for 150 ms: the tail and the loss probe are lost, so recovery starts with an RTO.
  long-lived-*.txt: -trials 1 -warmup 2 -fetches 5 -trace
      the same burst on every fetch of one connection, so the effects compound.
  mid-flight-hole-*.txt: -trials 8 -burst-drop 16 -burst-window 0 -trace
      16 packets lost, later packets delivered: SACK-driven RACK recovery, no RTO needed.
  tail-tlp-survives-*.txt: -trials 8 -burst-drop 100000 -burst-window 0 -burst-until-gap 3ms -trace
      everything after packet 1000 lost until the sender pauses 3 ms; the first packet after
      the pause (the loss probe) and everything later is delivered: RACK recovery after TLP.
superseded/: an earlier "drop for 4 ms" tail scenario (packets queued at the bottleneck kept
      ACKing past the 4 ms, so it was a mid-flight hole), and an earlier "drop exactly 40" scenario that also dropped the loss probe and the
      first RTO retransmission whenever the flight after packet 1000 was shorter than 40 packets.
